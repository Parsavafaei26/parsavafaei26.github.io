About

Why I started this

I'm Parsa, a biomedical engineer based in London. During my studies I worked on biomaterials and tissue engineering, and I kept seeing the same problem: the most exciting treatments are explained in papers that patients can't read, and in headlines that promise too much.

This site sits in between. Every card is researched from trial data and published sources, kept honest about the evidence, and drawn so anyone can follow it.
