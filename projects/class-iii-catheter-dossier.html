Fixing a rejected design dossier

The question

What I did

  • Owned the risk management section: wrote the risk management plan and the FMEA, including risk controls and residual risk scoring.
  • Reviewed the biological evaluation, checking that the biocompatibility tests matched a device in long-term contact with circulating blood.
