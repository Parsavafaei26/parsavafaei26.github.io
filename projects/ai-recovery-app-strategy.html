Bringing an AI recovery app to two markets

Planning how an AI-assisted app that guides patients after surgery could reach the market in the UK and the US.

P-122025
Project
A UK and US launch plan for a post-surgical recovery app
Context
MSc, University of Sheffield
2markets compared
~33%estimated cost saving from the chosen route

The question

What is the simplest, cheapest safe route to market for an AI app that influences clinical decisions?

What I did

  • Defined what the app does: a personalised care plan, data from home devices, automatic alerts to clinicians, and AI-assisted guidance.
  • Classified the product (Class IIa) and identified the software and usability standards it must meet.
  • Compared the UK route (UKCA marking) with the US route (FDA 510(k) against an existing cleared app).
  • Analysed launch costs and recommended launching in the UK first, with the intended use scoped to stay in Class IIa.

What I found

The recommended route was estimated to cut costs by about a third and make the launch simpler.
