Macrophage polarisation in atherosclerosis

Studying how macrophages, immune cells involved in artery plaque, change state, by measuring which genes they switch on.

P-042024–2025
Project
Gene expression in immune cells that drive plaque formation
Context
MSc project, University of Sheffield
qPCRgene expression
RNAextraction from cultured cells

The question

How does inflammatory signalling in macrophages change under conditions relevant to atherosclerosis?

What I did

  • Cultured and treated macrophages.
  • Extracted RNA and measured gene expression by qPCR.
  • Analysed expression and pathway data to investigate changes in inflammatory signalling, and wrote up the results.
  • Kept accurate experimental and inventory records so every sample was traceable.
