Drug response in glioblastoma cells

Testing how brain tumour cells respond to a drug at different doses, and writing a Python tool to count and size cells from microscope images.

The question

How do primary glioblastoma cells respond across a range of drug doses, in both survival and shape?

What I did

  • Designed and ran quantitative dose-response testing with an MTT assay in primary G7 glioblastoma cells.
  • Used fluorescence live-cell imaging and cytoskeletal staining to follow changes in cell shape.
  • Wrote and optimised, with AI assistance, a Python script that counts and sizes cells from the microscope images, replacing manual counting in ImageJ.
  • Maintained primary cell cultures under strict aseptic technique, with full documentation of methods and results.
